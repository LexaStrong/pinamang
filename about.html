STANDARDS & GOVERNANCE

Policies & Code of Conduct

Clear, positive standards ensuring a safe, supportive, and academically disciplined learning environment for every child.

Student Code of Conduct & Positive Discipline

We cultivate self-discipline, mutual courtesy, and high standards of moral behavior across all classroom and campus activities.

  • Strict punctuality for morning devotions, assembly, and all instructional periods.
  • Zero tolerance for bullying, harassment, insubordination, or disruptive behavior.
  • Restorative disciplinary system focused on reflection, counsel, and personal accountability.
  • Diligent care and respect for school property, library books, and science/ICT equipment.

Academic Standards & Assessment Protocols

Commitment to authentic intellectual effort and continuous evaluation preparing learners for outstanding BECE achievements.

  • Regular Continuous Assessment Tests (CATs) and mandatory daily homework assignments.
  • Strict anti-malpractice protocols for all internal assessments and national examinations.
  • Dedicated remedial coaching and one-on-one academic mentoring for every learner.
  • Comprehensive end-of-term academic reports and performance tracking issued to parents.

School Uniform & Grooming Standards

Wearing the official Pinamang uniform fosters equality, neatness, and a strong sense of communal pride.

  • Official Pinamang blue pattern uniform worn cleanly and neatly pressed on designated days.
  • Polished black leather school shoes paired with standard clean white socks.
  • Neat, modest haircuts conforming to Ghana Education Service (GES) basic school regulations.
  • Prescribed Pinamang physical education (P.E.) sports apparel for athletic and co-curricular days.

Child Safeguarding, Health & Campus Safety

A secure, fully gated campus with dedicated healthcare personnel, hygiene standards, and child safety guidelines.

  • Gated campus security with 24/7 personnel, visitor registration, and perimeter monitoring.
  • Well-equipped First Aid Sick Bay with trained healthcare attendants for immediate care.
  • Strict Child Protection and Safeguarding Code signed and adhered to by all faculty and staff.
  • Hygienic dining facilities and access to certified clean, treated potable drinking water.

Technology, Computing Lab & Device Policy

Structured ICT laboratory instruction aimed at computer literacy, STEM education, and ethical digital citizenship.

  • Supervised student access to computers, coding software, and safe educational research tools.
  • Personal mobile phones and non-educational electronic devices are strictly prohibited during school hours.
  • Comprehensive cyber safety education embedded within the computing and ICT syllabus.
  • Responsible handling and protection of all computer laboratory equipment and peripherals.

Parent-School Partnership & Communication

Collaborative engagement between parents, guardians, and educators to support every learner's holistic growth.

  • Active Parent-Teacher Association (PTA) general meetings and individual consultative open days.
  • Real-time performance tracking and school announcements accessible via the School Portal.
  • Open-door policy for scheduled appointments with school administration and class teachers.
  • Shared mutual responsibility for student attendance, homework monitoring, and moral character.
OUR JOURNEY

Over 3 Decades of Impact

Tracing the evolution of Pinamang Educational Complex into a cornerstone of quality education in Konongo.

1992 — Foundation

Establishment of Pinamang in Konongo

Founded in Konongo-Ahyiaem with a vision to provide disciplined, accessible, and high-standard academic training to the Asante Akim community.

2002 — Expansion

Comprehensive Basic Education Complex

Expanded to offer an integrated Crèche, Nursery, Kindergarten, and Primary School curriculum, laying early academic foundations for thousands of young learners.

2012 — JHS & STEM Excellence

Junior High School Accreditation & Lab Development

Inaugurated dedicated science and ICT laboratories, establishing Pinamang as a top-performing institution in the Basic Education Certificate Examination (BECE).

2026 & Beyond — Innovation

Modernized Learning & Character Leadership

Continuing to lead the region with modern facilities, qualified educators, digital student portals, and hundreds of accomplished alumni shaping Ghana and the world.
